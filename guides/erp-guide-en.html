Logging In & Finding
Your Way Around

Opening the ERP

The ERP lives in your browser — no installation needed. Open any browser (Chrome recommended) and type in the address bar:

  • At the office (on office Wi-Fi): http://192.168.1.135
  • From anywhere else: https://erp.sunandsun.com.tr
Tip Bookmark the address so you don't have to type it every time. In Chrome: press the ★ icon in the address bar.

Logging In

You'll see a login screen. Enter your email address and password, then click Sign In.

Your credentials are provided by your admin. If you've forgotten your password, ask them to reset it — there is no self-service password reset.

Remember Your account is personal. Don't share your password with colleagues — each person has their own login.

The Layout — Two Areas

Once logged in, the screen splits into two parts:

  • Left sidebar: the navigation menu. Click the icons to switch between tabs.
  • Main area (right): where everything happens — this changes depending on which tab you're on.

The 9 Tabs

The sidebar has these tabs (top to bottom):

  1. Dashboard — daily overview and stats
  2. Leads — your contact list
  3. Pipeline — visual deal tracker
  4. Cold Calls — automated phone outreach
  5. Email — bulk email campaigns
  6. Monday.com — full CRM contact database
  7. Contracts — PDF contract generator
  8. Price Quote — proposal builder
  9. Settings — (admin only)

Switching Languages

The ERP supports both Turkish and English. Look for the language toggle button at the top of the sidebar (it shows "TR" or "EN"). Click it to switch at any time — your preference is saved.

What You See When You Land Here

The Dashboard is the first thing you see after logging in. Think of it as your daily newspaper — all the important headlines in one place, before you dive into the detail.

The Stat Cards (Top Row)

Four big numbers greet you at the top:

  • Total Leads — every contact currently in the system
  • Qualified Leads — contacts confirmed as potential clients
  • Won Deals — closed sales
  • Average Score — the average lead quality score (0–100)

Below those you'll also see Win Rate (percentage of leads converted), Score Distribution (how many leads are hot/warm/cold), and Call Activity.

The Conversion Funnel

The funnel chart shows how leads move through the sales stages. The top is wide (lots of new leads), and it narrows as you go down to "Won". A very narrow funnel means many leads are dropping off early — worth investigating.

Hot Leads

Scroll to the bottom of the Dashboard to find the Hot Leads section — these are the contacts with the highest lead scores. They should be your priority calls of the day. Click any card to open their full profile.

Remember The Dashboard is read-only. You can see everything but change nothing here. To update a lead, go to the Leads tab.
Tip Make it a habit to check the Dashboard every morning before anything else. It takes 30 seconds and keeps you focused on the right leads.

Finding a Lead

The Leads tab shows all contacts in the system. The list can be long — here's how to find who you're looking for fast:

  1. Click Leads in the sidebar.
  2. Type a name or company in the search bar at the top.
  3. Use the filter dropdowns (Industry, Status, City) to narrow things down further.
  4. Click any row to open that contact's full profile.

Inside a Lead Profile

When you open a lead, you see their complete card:

  • Contact info: email, phone, LinkedIn URL
  • Company details: industry, city, company size, source
  • Lead Score: a number from 0–100. Higher = hotter. Scores above 70 are your priority.
  • Status: where this lead is in the sales process
  • Client Needs: a checklist of what they're looking for
  • Notes: a free-text area for logging everything you know
  • Call History: a log of past automated calls

Updating a Lead's Status

This is the most important thing you do in the Leads tab. Always update it after each interaction:

  1. Open the lead profile.
  2. Find the Status dropdown inside the profile.
  3. Select the new stage from the list.
  4. The Pipeline board updates automatically.

The status stages in order are:

  • New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal Sent → Negotiation → Won or Lost

Adding Notes

The Notes field is your memory. After every call, email, or meeting, write a quick summary here. Future you — and your colleagues — will thank you.

  1. Open the lead profile.
  2. Click inside the Notes text area.
  3. Type your note. Be specific: date, what was discussed, next step.
  4. Click Save (or it may auto-save — look for the confirmation).
Tip A good note looks like this: "02/06/2026 — Called, spoke to Mehmet. Interested in KOSGEB support. Will send proposal by Friday. Follow up Monday."
Don't Do This You cannot delete leads — that's an admin action. If a lead needs to be removed, contact your admin.

What Is a Kanban Board?

The Pipeline is a visual board where each column represents a stage in your sales process. Each lead appears as a card in the column that matches their current status. You can see at a glance exactly where every deal stands.

The 7 Columns (Left to Right)

  • New — just added to the system, no contact made yet
  • Contacted — you've reached out at least once
  • Qualified — confirmed they have a real need and budget
  • Proposal Sent — a quote or contract has been sent
  • Negotiation — actively discussing terms
  • Won — deal closed, contract signed 🎉
  • Lost — didn't close, for now

Reading the Cards

Each card shows the lead's name, company, and their top needs as small badges. Click any card to go straight to their full profile.

Each column also shows a count of how many leads are in that stage. If "Proposal Sent" has 8 leads and "Won" has 1, that's a conversation worth having.

Tip Use the Pipeline view during weekly team meetings. It takes one look to see who's stuck and who's moving forward.
Remember Cards only move when you update the lead's Status in the lead profile. The pipeline reflects the status — it doesn't update itself automatically.

What Are Automated Cold Calls?

The Cold Calls tab connects to an automated calling system. When you click the Call button next to a lead, the system places the call on your behalf. You can monitor the call in real time and, if a transcript is captured, read it afterwards.

The Stats at the Top

Four counters at the top of the page tell you:

  • Total Calls — all calls ever made
  • Completed — calls that connected and finished
  • No Answer — calls with no pick-up
  • Voicemail — calls that went to voicemail

Making a Call

  1. Click Cold Calls in the sidebar.
  2. Find the lead in the list (only leads with phone numbers appear here).
  3. Check the Call count next to their name — it shows how many times they've been called and the last call result.
  4. Click the Call button.
  5. A call window pops up showing the lead's name, phone number, and live status.
  6. When the call ends, any captured transcript appears in the window.
  7. Close the window when you're done.

Call Status Badges

Each lead in the list shows a colored badge for their last call result:

  • Completed — successful call
  • No Answer — no pick-up
  • Voicemail — left voicemail
  • No badge — not yet called
Don't Do This If you see a warning banner saying the calling system isn't configured, don't try to make calls — contact your admin to set it up first.

The Email Tab at a Glance

The Email tab lets you send one message to many contacts at once. You can write a custom email or use a pre-written template. The system tracks who received it and the success rate.

Sending a Campaign — Step by Step

  1. Click Email in the sidebar.
  2. Use a Quick Reply Template button to auto-fill a pre-written message, or type your own Subject and Body.
  3. Select your Signature from the dropdown (Merve, Şura, Ahmet, or Esra). The signature is appended automatically.
  4. Filter your Recipients: choose by Status, Industry, and whether they have a valid email address.
  5. Click Preview to see exactly how the email will look before it goes out.
  6. Click Send.

Filtering Recipients

You'll almost never want to email every single lead. Use the filters to target the right group:

  • Status filter: for example, only email "Qualified" leads
  • Industry filter: target a specific sector
  • Valid email only: check this to skip contacts with no email address
Tip Always filter out "Lost" leads before sending. Emailing people who already said no is bad for your sender reputation and annoying for them.

Checking Campaign History

Scroll down on the Email page to see Campaign History. Each past campaign shows:

  • Date sent
  • Number of recipients
  • Success rate (delivered vs. bounced)
Don't Do This You cannot unsend an email. Always click Preview before Send. Double-check the subject line especially — a typo in the subject line goes to everyone.

What Is the Monday.com Tab?

This tab connects directly to the company's Monday.com account. It shows your full client and prospect database — contacts (individual people) and companies (organizations). It's a bigger, richer database than the Leads tab.

Contacts vs. Companies

At the top of the tab, two buttons let you switch:

  • Contacts — individual people (name, email, phone, job title)
  • Companies — organizations (company name, email, employee count)

Searching & Filtering

Use the filter panel to find exactly who you need:

  • Type in Name, Email, or Phone to search
  • Set an Employee Count range to filter companies by size
  • Check Show Only Valid Email to hide contacts without a working email

Sending a Bulk Email from CRM

You can email a selected group directly from this tab:

  1. Check the box next to each contact (or company) you want to email. You can check multiple.
  2. Click the Send Bulk button that appears.
  3. A compose window opens — fill in Subject, Body (or pick a template), and choose a Signature.
  4. Click Send.

Syncing Bounces

Sometimes emails bounce (invalid addresses). Click Sync Bounces to pull in the latest bounce data from the email system. Bounced addresses get flagged so they don't receive future campaigns.

Remember Contacts in this tab come from Monday.com. If someone you expect to see is missing, they need to be added in Monday.com first — the ERP syncs from there.
Tip Use the Find Duplicates button occasionally to clean up the database. It highlights contacts that appear more than once.

What the Contracts Tab Does

The Contracts tab lets you fill out a pre-built contract template and download a ready-to-sign PDF in minutes. All the legal text is already written — you just fill in the blanks.

Generating a Contract — Step by Step

  1. Click Contracts in the sidebar.
  2. Select the Company from the dropdown. Type to search if the list is long.
  3. Select the Template — for example "KOSGEB Dijital Dönüşüm" or "Tekil Danışmanlık".
  4. The form updates to show the fields for that template. Fill them all in (see below).
  5. Click Generate PDF (or Generate Word if you need an editable file).
  6. The file downloads automatically to your Downloads folder.

The Form Fields — What You'll Fill In

Depending on the template, you'll see fields like:

  • Party 1 / Party 2 / Party 3: name, tax number, tax office, address — the signing parties
  • Program Name: what service/program the contract covers
  • Financier: the funding body (e.g., KOSGEB)
  • Service Fee: the amount in Turkish Lira
  • Deadline: number of days (type just the number, e.g., 60)
  • Success Bonus: percentage fee upon project approval
  • Success Bonus Type: whether the bonus is "approved support", "approved loan", or "provided benefit"
  • Contract Date: select from the calendar

Using OCR to Scan a Document

For Party 2 and Party 3, instead of typing manually you can scan a tax certificate or ID document:

  1. Click Upload Image next to Party 2 (or Party 3).
  2. Select a photo or scanned image of the document (JPG, PNG).
  3. Wait a few seconds — the system reads the document and auto-fills the fields.
  4. Review every field carefully and correct any mistakes before proceeding.
Tip OCR is very accurate but not perfect. Always read through the auto-filled fields before generating the contract — especially tax numbers and addresses.

Adding a Payment Schedule (Annex-1)

Most contracts include a payment schedule table. Here's how to add it:

  1. Scroll to the Annex-1 / Payment Schedule section on the right side of the form.
  2. Click + Add Row to add a payment installment.
  3. For each row: select the Subject (type of payment) from the dropdown, pick the Date, and type the Amount.
  4. Add as many rows as needed.
  5. The table will be included in the generated PDF automatically.
Remember Enter dates in the format dd/mm/yyyy. Enter amounts as numbers only — no "TL" or commas needed, the system formats them automatically.
Don't Do This You cannot upload new templates or delete existing ones — that's an admin task. If you need a new contract template, ask your admin.

What the Price Quote Tab Does

The Price Quote tab (called Fiyat Teklifi in Turkish) builds a professional, Canva-powered proposal PDF. You pick a presentation design, fill in your pricing, and download a polished PDF to share with the client — all in under 5 minutes.

Building a Quote — Step by Step

  1. Click Price Quote (or Fiyat Teklifi) in the sidebar.
  2. Select a Presentation from the category buttons. Each one is a different Canva design.
  3. Type the Client Name — this becomes the filename of the downloaded PDF (e.g., "Acme_Corp" → Acme_Corp.pdf).
  4. Choose a Theme: Blue or Green.
  5. Choose a Mode: Standard or With Discount (see below).
  6. Fill in the quote cards (up to 3 options).
  7. Click Generate PDF.

Standard Mode vs. Discount Mode

  • Standard: shows your regular prices straightforwardly.
  • With Discount: shows a crossed-out original price and the discounted price side-by-side. Great for showing a client they're getting a deal.

The Quote Cards

You can include up to 3 service options in one quote. Each card has:

  • Title — the name of the service/program
  • Down Payment — upfront fee (or discounted price in Discount mode)
  • Original Price — (Discount mode only) the price before discount
  • Success Fee 1 & 2 — percentage fee upon project success
  • Note — any extra info for this option
Tip Offering two options (e.g., a basic and a premium package) gives clients a sense of choice and often leads to better conversions than a single take-it-or-leave-it price.
Tip Use the Client Name field carefully — avoid special characters. Type the company name as you want it to appear in the filename.
Don't Do This You cannot add or remove presentations from the list — that's an admin task. If you need a new presentation type, ask your admin.
